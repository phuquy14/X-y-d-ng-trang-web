About Our

TEA STATION

Once upon a time we only had a couple of teapots and enjoyed tea only with our family. Over time, we were able to afford to buy a small tea plantation.

Now we have a whole teahouse and we delight our guests with delicious cold and hot tea. We serve black long tea, relaxing green tea, herbal infusions with spices and many others.
